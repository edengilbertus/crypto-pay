# Crypto Pay — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-09-14_

## Summary
Crypto Pay is a **self-custody companion app**. It connects to wallets you already use (via WalletConnect / Reown). **We never collect or store your private keys, seed phrases, or passwords.**

## What we process
- **Wallet public address** and chain ID after you approve a connection.
- **On-chain balances and transactions** for that public address (read from public RPC providers such as Alchemy).
- **Local app preferences** needed to run the app on your device.

## What we do **not** do
- We do **not** store private keys or seed phrases.
- We do **not** require an account or login for core wallet features.
- We do **not** sell your personal data.

## Third parties
| Service | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Reown / WalletConnect** | Wallet connection sessions |
| **Alchemy** | Blockchain RPC (balances, gas, tx status) |
| **CoinGecko** | Public token prices |

Each provider has its own privacy policy.

## Your controls
- Disconnect your wallet at any time in the app.
- Revoke the connection in your wallet app.
- Uninstall the app to remove local data.
